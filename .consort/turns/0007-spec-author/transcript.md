## Final reasoning

I wrote the five acceptance criteria for S1-file-stock-record to its `acs/` folder, and the story self-check passes:

- **AC1-file-record-appears:** a valid submission files the record without a page reload.
- **AC2-missing-required-field-inline:** a blank required field shows an error next to it, naming the field.
- **AC3-invalid-quantity-inline:** an invalid quantity shows an error naming the field.
- **AC4-same-key-updates-in-place:** filing the same SKU and location again updates the record, with no duplicate.
- **AC5-optional-tracking-code-omitted:** a record filed without a tracking code is accepted and stored empty, so S2 and S3 can show it as "not tracked".

I also wrote the S1 section of `feature-spec.md` and added a data-semantics section there. Four open questions are logged with my recommended answers for the Product Owner to confirm:
- **Re-filing the same SKU and location:** update the existing record. AC4 assumes this.
- **Tracking code:** optional.
- **Par level:** not entered in F1; it shows as "not tracked".
- **Quantity 0:** allowed.
